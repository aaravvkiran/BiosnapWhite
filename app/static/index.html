Protecting Our Local Ecosystems

Invasive species pose one of the greatest threats to global biodiversity, second only to habitat destruction. They outcompete native plants and animals, alter soil chemistry, and can cause significant economic damage.

Our Mission

To empower communities with accessible technology to rapidly identify, report, and manage invasive species before they establish permanent footholds in native habitats.

How it works
